Bioblurb

Stephen is a Partner Solutions Engineer at DataHub, where he helps design cross-platform data context layers—column-level lineage, observability, and governance—that support agentic workloads for strategic technology and cloud partners. Before DataHub, he spent years designing and deploying AI/ML systems: agentic pipelines in FIPS-compliant environments at Research Triangle Institute, high-throughput FastAPI backends scaling to 3M+ daily transactions, and hybrid full-text/vector retrieval systems that boosted search relevance by 80%. Earlier still, he was a Solutions Architect at MongoDB and a backend engineer building event-driven data pipelines for clinical and genomics research. He's passionate about building AI-driven systems that are reliable, scalable, and built with privacy and security in mind.
